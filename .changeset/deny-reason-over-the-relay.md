A phone paired straight to the Mac can say why it denies a held call, and the agent is told that reason as it is from the workspace. A phone or the Mac can also answer `never`, which denies the call and puts up a deny rule for the next identical one, carrying the same reason, to place and sign.

A spoken rule on an enrolled machine is drafted by the workspace, whose plan decides whether its model reads it, and checked by the runtime's own validator; with no account or offline the machine's own grammar reads it. The fallback to a local Claude Code model is gone.
