Say a rule and choose where it holds. Hold Control and Option on the Mac, say the rule, and let go: the voice turns it into a proposed rule, now able to name branches and paths as well as an action, and the Mac shows it with the places it could hold, the whole Mac or one of your repositories. The place you pick is what Touch ID signs, a whole Mac rule goes in `~/.memnox/machine.policies.toml`, and a repository rule is narrowed to that folder, so it no longer applies everywhere just because its file is registered.
