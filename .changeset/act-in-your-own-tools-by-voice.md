The voice reads and acts in your own tools in your own name rather than a bot's: Slack, mail, documents, GitHub and Linear, read at once, and a DM, an email or a review reply drawn the way that tool draws it and sent only after Touch ID or Face ID. Working alone, you can use any tool; in a team, your own mail, calendar and documents, and the team's tools once an admin connected them. Ask for something in a tool you have not used yet and its icon appears in the notch: one tap signs you in, and the voice carries on with what you asked. Name the tool, as in "comment on the GitHub pull request", and that tool is used and no other, whichever tool it is. An account nothing ties to you is named in the notch with Not me beside it and asks nothing more, since the first thing you send from it with Touch ID ties it to you. Ask without the words and the voice drafts them for you to read on the card, and who it goes to is found among the people in that tool rather than written as heard. A company that holds an app back is named rather than leaving it to fail.
