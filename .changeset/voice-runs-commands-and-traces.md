Any memnox command can be asked for by voice: what reads runs at once, and what changes something waits for Touch ID. `memnox traces` shows an agent's session or Memnox answering a question as a waterfall of what ran, for how long and what failed, and sends it to Logfire or any OpenTelemetry collector with `--otlp`.
