What connected tools such as Slack, Notion and Linear said that nobody has approved yet now reaches the agent as context. The workspace memory keeps it in its own `unconfirmed` list, the `memory` and `brief` session tools answer it apart from the facts under a warning, and the session hook says at most one per call, each opening with that nobody approved it and naming where it was read and who said it, with the untrusted warning where its source was outside the workspace. No verdict reads it, and nothing in it is ever described as settled.
