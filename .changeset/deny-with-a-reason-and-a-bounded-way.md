`memnox protect` asks about databases on their own rather than as a CLI: a DELETE or UPDATE with no WHERE, a TRUNCATE, a DROP or a schema change is held on every database client, while a write bounded by a WHERE on a local database goes through, and any change to a database on another host still asks. The refusal names the table and the bounded way forward, such as only the test orders. A person who denies a held call from the console, the workspace or a phone can say why, and the agent is told their reason beside that way forward. Existing `cli-ask` rules are left as they were written; run `memnox protect` again to take the new database rules.
