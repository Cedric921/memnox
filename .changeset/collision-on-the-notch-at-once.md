A write held off because another agent has the same lines now reaches the notch in about a fifth of a second, and where that agent is on another machine in the workspace the voice offers to ask its owner: say "ask Ana if I can have it" and her voice tells her at once, her spoken give, wait or keep comes straight back to yours, and a give hands the lines to your agent so its next write goes through. The daemon now holds the person's workspace stream open itself, so incidents, exception requests and calls still short of their approvals are said the moment they reach someone who can act on them, and an incident can be taken and an exception refused by voice.
