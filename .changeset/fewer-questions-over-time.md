Memnox asks less as it learns, and says so. An allow rule can now name the ask rules it stands in for (`relaxes`), because an allow beside an ask was always still asked about: `memnox next --hand-over` writes allows that actually stop the asking, never over a deny and never over a rule the workspace published. A held call takes a fourth answer, "From now on", on the Mac and the iPhone: the call runs once and a narrow rule for that agent, action and target goes on a card the person places and signs. A new agent on probation no longer asks before writing inside its repository, because the milestone kept before its first write puts that back; writes outside the repository, deletes and outward actions still ask. A finished turn now says what it came to, "It ran 47 actions and asked you 1 time", and `memnox next` shows questions per week against work per week, with the sessions that asked nothing.
