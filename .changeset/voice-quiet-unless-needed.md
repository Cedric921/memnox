The voice stays quiet after a conversation until it is called, and speaks unasked only for what needs the person: a held call, an agent's question, a paused session, a failed command or a watch they set. A login or onboarding that worked is shown rather than said, coming back to the Mac is greeted only when something waits, and a notice no longer ends in an offer. The voice can also read the workspace memory this Mac pulled and ask the workspace for a brief.
