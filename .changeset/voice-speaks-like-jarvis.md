The voice speaks in the manner of J.A.R.V.I.S.: formal, precise and composed, with a light British turn of phrase, a calm recommendation whenever something is wrong, and a dry line only now and then. `memnox voice address` sets how it addresses you, such as "sir" or your name, and with nothing set it uses no form of address rather than guessing one.
