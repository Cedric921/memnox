The voice starts Claude Code, or any agent, in a new terminal inside VS Code, Cursor or Windsurf when the person names the editor, through the Memnox extension, and remembers that place for the next start. A turn always ends, so a request the brain held on twice is answered out loud rather than leaving the orb silent. A send the Mac cannot type is said once with its real reason, is not tried again the same way, and is offered as a new session where the person works. The voice keeps to the place the person named, reads "poem" as prompt and "tent file" as the changed files, and hears "so you cannot open VS Code" as a request. A session recorded under both its hosted id and the agent's own conversation id is one session, so its edits show beside its network, and the files it changed are listed from every repository it wrote in. Each snapshot of the working tree uses a scratch index of its own, since a lock left on the shared one made every later comparison in that repository come back empty.
