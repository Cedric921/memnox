A paired phone now answers every yes with Face ID. While a phone is paired, every held call and proposal it may answer names it as `answerOn`, so the Mac leaves a spoken yes to that phone rather than Touch ID and says where to answer it. Something a phone asked the Mac to do now names that phone as its origin, so it waits for that phone's Face ID whether or not the phone still holds the voice, and the Mac never declines it as unasked. A phone talking from the island with its app away is woken for what waits even while its tunnel is open for the turn, and a wake held back by the one a minute spacing is sent once the minute is up rather than never.
