Acting as yourself needs no Memnox account. The voice and a paired phone now reach the MCP servers your agents are configured with and the command line tools you are signed in to, such as gh, stripe or psql, straight from this Mac: reads run at once, anything that changes something waits on your Touch ID, and every call passes the same rules, pauses and limits an agent's call does. Connecting a tool from the catalogue opens only that tool's own sign in, because the Mac registers a private personal space of its own the first time it needs one, and pairing a phone works the same way. Signing in later moves what you connected and your paired phone into your account, so nothing is connected or paired twice.
