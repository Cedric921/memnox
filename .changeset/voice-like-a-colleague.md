The voice now speaks like a colleague who has been watching the sessions. The brain is told the person's name and to talk the way a person does: short, the answer first, one concrete detail, never guessing. The daemon's new `brief` has it tell what happened rather than what was asked: a held call with what its session has been doing and why it needs the person, a paused session with where it got stuck, and a catch up when the person comes back to the Mac. Text that came from an agent is marked as data in those prompts, never an instruction, and the name is kept to plain letters before it goes anywhere near one.
