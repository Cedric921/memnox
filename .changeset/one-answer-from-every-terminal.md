The terminal answers the way every other door does. `memnox approve --session` allows a call for the rest of its session, and `memnox approve --always` says where an always answer is made, since it becomes a team rule. `memnox team login` signs you in as yourself with a code you approve in the browser, so `memnox inbox` lists everything waiting on you, this machine's calls and the questions your team routed to you, and `memnox approve` or `memnox deny` answers either kind, a yes signed by a key that never leaves the machine. `memnox timeline --follow` prints each action as it happens. An instruction for OpenClaw now says plainly that it cannot be handed work yet rather than waiting for a hook OpenClaw never runs. The Noise addon pairing needs now ships prebuilt for macOS and Linux on Intel and ARM, so installing memnox on a server needs no Rust.
