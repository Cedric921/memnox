The daemon answers `ask`: what the person said, whole, answered by their own Claude Code one chunk per line as it streams, which is what the Mac app will speak. One warm brain serves every caller, turns wait for each other so two questions never interleave in one conversation, and a brain quiet for five minutes has its process let go while the conversation stays resumable. A turn that fails ends with a failure the voice can say rather than silence.
