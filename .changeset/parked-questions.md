A question nobody answers in time is parked for sixteen hours instead of dropped. The agent is told to carry on with what does not need it, a retry is answered at once rather than asked again, and a yes given later from the phone, the terminal or the workspace lets the retry through. `memnox approvals` shows parked questions and how long they have waited.
