The voice signs the person in to Memnox Cloud itself with `memnox_sign_in`, the same sign in as the Account row, rather than sending them to a menu or a terminal. It counts the tools their workspace connected from the same list the Tools panel shows, never calls an unverified account one still to confirm, reads only their own pull requests when asked for theirs, keeps dictated words such as "gate status" as said, writes a draft's label for the screen, and says they for anyone whose pronouns it was not told.
